One joke about Monday mornings, at most 200 characters, written for make.fun's "Monday Morning Punchlines" bounty. A judge model compares the entries two at a time and keeps the funnier one.

The entry:

> I tried to unsubscribe from Monday morning. It replied: 'Your free trial of the weekend has ended.'

The angle: Monday morning as a subscription service — the weekend was only ever a free trial, and Monday is the renewal notice. A real punchline with a twist, not a complaint about Mondays, and not an alarm-clock cliche.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (1–200 characters, UTF-8).
